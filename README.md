# Anavaratha

**Intuitui Labs** · systems research · Sri Lanka  
**Entrance optic:** syntropy-first (aliveness, generative form, goodness-as-form-signal) — then metabolism under pressure, then critical refusal of regeneration-as-cover.

This repository is the working surface for Anavaratha. It is **not** a product, not a venture engine (see Peace Valley), and not an EdLove delivery track.

## What this is studying

How material–institutional–people–ecosystem patterns **compose usable order** (capability that can open the next cycle) — and only after that, how pressure, trapped residuals, and “regen” talk interrupt or disguise that composition.

Public living-doc premise (studio site) remains thin and Stage 02 Sensemaking. This repo holds internal research design and desk evidence. **No claim of readiness to intervene.**
