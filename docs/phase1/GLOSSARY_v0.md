# Anavaratha — Glossary (v0)
Operational definitions for research design. Not public language locks.

| Term | Working definition | Still open |
|------|-------------------|------------|
| **Capability** | Durable capacity of a material, institutional, human, or ecological system to perform valued functions under stress. | Valued *by whom*? Whose functions count? |
| **Use** | Acts that spend capability (extraction, labor, policy execution, cultivation, circulation). | Distinguishing productive vs extractive use without moralizing early. |
| **Residuality** | What remains after use: stocks, wastes, skills, memories, degraded or latent forms. Residuals can enable or block regeneration. | Inventory categories for Sri Lanka sites. |
| **Regeneration** | Processes that restore, recompose, or substitute capability from residuals or new inputs. | Natural vs institutional regeneration; time scales. |
| **Pressure** | Conditions that accelerate capability loss or constrain regeneration (climatic, market, fiscal, infrastructural, political). | Thresholds vs continuous gradients. |
| **Unit of analysis** | The bounded entity we observe (a watershed, a cooperative, a material flow, a school plant, a coastal stretch). | Site hypotheses gated by Naveen. |
| **Evidence** | Traceable observations that support or kill a question (field notes, material-flow accounts, institutional docs, interviews, spatial layers). | What does *not* count yet (anecdotes alone, uncited stats). |
| **Stage exit (Sensemaking)** | Clear questions, boundaries, evidence catalog, and kill criteria — still no claim of readiness to act. | Exact bar TBD with Naveen. |
