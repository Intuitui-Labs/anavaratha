## Vs Agri / Rural (and Knowledge Infrastructure) delivery domains

Studio sectoral domains (Education, SME, Agriculture & Rural, Governance, Knowledge Infrastructure) are **delivery** lanes. Anavaratha is an **upstream sensemaking lens**:

- It may later *inform* Agri/Rural or Knowledge Infrastructure designs.
- It is **not** itself an Agri/Rural delivery program, extension service, or sector product.
- Questions that collapse into “how do we deliver X to farms/villages?” without a capability-cycle evidence path belong in those domains, not here.

## Constellation test (for every live question)

Before keeping a question, assign it:

1. **Anavaratha** — capability / use / residual / regeneration under pressure  
2. **Peace Valley** — venture / economy / enterprise engine  
3. **EdLove / Gurudevi** — education care delivery  
4. **Agri/Rural or other delivery domain** — sector implementation  

If assignment is ambiguous, park until CoS clarifies — do not double-count.
