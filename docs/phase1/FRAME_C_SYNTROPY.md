## Frame C in our words

**Syntropy** here means: patterned increase of usable order, coherence, and generative capacity in a material–institutional–people–ecosystem field — not vibes, not branding, not “positive thinking.”

**Frame C says:**
1. First ask what makes Sri Lankan material–institutional–ecological patterns **generative** — where capability is composed, gifted, tended, and made more alive across cycles.
2. Beauty and goodness are not decorations on the research; they are **signals of form** — when a cascade, craft, institution, or ecological relation *works as living form*, that form is evidence of syntropy.
3. Use → residual → regeneration is still real, but under C the primary object is **what is being generated**, not what is being prevented from dying.
4. Only after a generative pattern is named does the underside matter: which pressures threaten it, which residuals trap it, which “regen” talk covers loss of aliveness.

**C is not:**
- Ignoring collapse, scarcity, or institutional failure
- Peace Valley venture language (boundary lock stands)
- EdLove care delivery
- A public rebrand of the living-doc tagline

**C is:**
- A **research priority** and entrance optic: start from life that works, then study how it is strained
