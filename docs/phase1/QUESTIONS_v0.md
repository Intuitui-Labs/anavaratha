# Anavaratha — Research questions (v0)
Ranked. Kill list included. No fieldwork until Naveen greenlights.

## Keep (ranked)

1. **Capability under what pressure?** Which pressure types most visibly shorten “time to act” for Sri Lankan material–institutional–ecological systems we can actually observe?
2. **Residuals that regenerate vs residuals that trap.** For selected material or institutional cycles, which residuals become feedstock for regeneration and which lock systems into degradation?
3. **Institutional memory as residual.** How do formal and informal institutions store (or lose) operational capability after personnel, funding, or policy turnover?
4. **Material flow visibility.** Where are material cycles (use → residual → return) legible enough for evidence without inventing data infrastructure we do not have?
5. **People as carriers of capability.** How do skilled people move capability across sites (migration, apprenticeship, diaspora, project churn), and what remains when they leave?
6. **Ecosystem buffers.** Which ecological buffers (soil, water, canopy, coastal) buy time under climatic or extractive pressure in candidate geographies?
7. **Evidence sufficiency for stage exit.** What minimum evidence set would justify leaving Sensemaking without claiming intervention readiness?
8. **Constellation boundary test.** For any candidate question, does it belong to Anavaratha, Peace Valley (venture/economy), Agri/Rural delivery, or EdLove — and why?
