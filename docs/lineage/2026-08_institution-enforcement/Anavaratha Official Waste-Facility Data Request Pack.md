# Anavaratha Official Waste-Facility Data Request Pack

**Purpose:** Request current, historical, and status-transition data needed to verify facility-capacity reality and hidden disposal. These are drafts for user review and submission; no requests are sent automatically.

## Common request

Please provide, preferably in spreadsheet or GIS format, the complete list of municipal solid-waste, composting, material-recovery, recycling, transfer, landfill, dumpsite, waste-to-energy, clinical-waste, and other relevant waste facilities under your authority or records. For each facility please provide official name, alternate names, owner, operator, local authority, province, district, GN/ward, latitude/longitude or polygon, facility type, accepted waste stream, design capacity, installed capacity, permitted capacity, actual monthly intake, actual throughput, product output, reject/residual output, operating days, downtime, maintenance, staffing, weighing method, source-separation arrangements, residual destination, licence/permit, EIA/IEE, inspection date, complaints, fires, odour/leachate incidents, enforcement, rehabilitation, closure, and post-closure monitoring status for 2019–2026.

Please identify the source document, page/table, responsible unit, update date, data dictionary, known missing fields, and whether each value is measured, estimated, reported, planned, or target-based. Please state whether the record may be publicly released and, if not, the legal or administrative basis for restricted access.

## CEA request

Please provide the current national registry or inventory of licensed and inspected solid-waste treatment and disposal facilities; facility licences and renewal status; EIA/IEE approvals; inspection and enforcement records; Pollution Source Inventory access route or export; records of open-dump closure and rehabilitation; environmental monitoring records; and the current list of facilities classified as sanitary landfill, controlled dump, open dump, composting, recycling, transfer, or waste-to-energy.

Please clarify whether the Pollution Source Inventory is operational, which waste-facility fields it contains, its geographic coverage, the last update date, and the procedure for obtaining a research extract.

## Western Province WMA request

Please provide the raw Western Province solid-waste database or a research export covering all 49 local authorities, including the data dictionary, facility IDs, local-authority participation list, monthly records from 2019–2026, missingness report, validation procedure, user/audit logs, and public-access policy. Please provide the current status of all 28 composting facilities, all local-authority disposal sites, private disposal contracts, transfer stations, Karadiyana, Dikkowita, Kalutara, and other WMA-managed facilities.

Please provide the current implementation status of the Western Province Solid Waste Management Master Plan, including project schedules, budgets, procurement, construction, commissioning, operating records, maintenance, residual pathways, and closure of local-authority dumpsites.

## Local-authority request

For each local authority, please provide all waste collection zones, service coverage, fleet inventory and uptime, staffing and absenteeism, collection tonnage, transfer points, disposal/treatment destinations, facility operating records, complaints, seasonal overflow locations, open burning or illegal dumping reports, and any temporary disposal or emergency diversion from 2019–2026.

Please identify whether the local authority operates a disposal site, outsources disposal, uses a shared site, or transfers waste to a provincial or private facility. Please provide site polygons or coordinates and copies of current permits, agreements, inspection reports, and closure/rehabilitation documents.

## SLLRDC and facility-operator request

Please provide operating and maintenance records for Kerawalapitiya, Muthurajawela, and related waste-park facilities, including accepted waste, treatment, rejects, residuals, leachate, fire/odour incidents, equipment uptime, contractor arrangements, and current operating status. Please distinguish temporary emergency disposal areas from permanent facilities and provide their closure or remediation status.

## National Solid Waste Management Support Center request

Please provide national local-authority support records, facility inventories, training and technical-assistance records, project completion and persistence evaluations, current data systems, and any national or provincial facility-capacity datasets held by the Center. Please identify which facilities received equipment or capital support and whether post-commissioning operation was evaluated.

## Evidence and privacy protocol

No personal information is required. Requests should seek aggregated operational and facility records, not names of individual workers or complainants. Any restricted record should be logged as an access barrier, with the institution, request date, response date, and reason for restriction preserved in Anavaratha’s evidence register.
