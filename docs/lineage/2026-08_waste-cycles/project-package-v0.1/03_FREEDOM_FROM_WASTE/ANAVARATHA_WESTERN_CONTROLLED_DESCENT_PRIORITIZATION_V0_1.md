# Western Province Controlled-Descent Prioritization

**Status:** Desk-based prioritization only; not a fieldwork authorization or allegation list. **Date:** 22 August 2026.

## Selection logic

A case is prioritized only when four conditions are met: a documented system type; a specific unresolved mechanism; a defensible spatial lead; and a feasible verification question. Cases are not selected from OSM density, proximity to water, public suspicion, or presumed illegality alone.

| Priority | Candidate system | Prior evidence | Hypothesis | Minimum desk gate before fieldwork | Field question |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Karadiyana / Site A | Conflicting formal and material descriptions; refurbishment and equipment handover | A rehabilitated centralized site may still contain uncontrolled mixed-waste, residual, or leachate pathways | Current polygon, permit, inspection, weighbridge, equipment uptime, leachate/fire records | What enters, what is processed, what remains, and where does it go? |
| 2 | Kerawalapitiya and adjacent emergency area | Historical temporary dumping beside formal compost/waste-park facility | Emergency disposal may leave a persistent or unrecorded residual area | Exact adjacent-area boundary, closure/remediation evidence, current land use | Did the temporary pathway end, and was the residual burden removed or contained? |
| 3 | Dompe | Formal CEA sanitary-landfill label and compost-facility role | A formal label may conceal current status or incomplete residual controls | Current operator, permit, operating logs, monitoring and site polygon | Is current operation demonstrably sanitary and traceable? |
| 4 | Dense-corridor numbered map lead | WMA Figure 2-10 large/medium/minor open-dump symbol near urban local authorities | Land scarcity and transfer dependence may generate ground unloading or hidden overflow | Georeference symbol, identify LA, obtain current status and route records | Is the map lead closed, transferred, renamed, or still receiving waste? |
| 5 | Southern/interior numbered map lead | WMA Figure 2-10 dispersed small/minor symbols | Lower-density systems may have different land, fleet, and seasonal disposal constraints | Georeference symbol, confirm LA and current facility records | Does dispersed service lead to remote or seasonal dumping? |

## Required pre-field evidence packet

The selected case must have a source-traceable site polygon or uncertainty-bounded map lead; current status response from responsible authority; at least one current or recent operational/inspection record; documented accepted stream; known receiving and residual route; and a list of unresolved questions. If these are not obtained, the case remains a desk-based verification lead.

## Output order

The first output should be a currentness and identity reconciliation. The second should be a pathway map. The third should be a capacity and persistence audit. The fourth should be a field protocol tailored to the unresolved mechanism. No locality should be presented as a confirmed illegal dump until source and field evidence support that claim.
