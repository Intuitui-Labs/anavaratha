## Start here

Anavaratha is organized around three distinct but connected domains:

1. **Freedom from Waste** — reducing unmanaged residual burdens, exposure, leakage, accumulation, unsafe work, and institutional abandonment.
2. **Material Afterlives** — studying latent utility, reuse, repair, exchange, refurbishment, salvage, and post-use circulation before or beyond waste.
3. **Ecological Flourishing** — studying ecological capability, regeneration, restoration, biodiversity, soils, forests, wetlands, waterways, and stewardship.

The project uses thermodynamic humility, material-flow analysis, industrial ecology, ecological metabolism, information, capability, political ecology, environmental justice, resilience, and GIS. These frames are connected but not collapsed into one score or one theory.

## Evidence-status rules

The package distinguishes **observed/measured**, **audited**, **reported**, **estimated/modelled**, **planned/target**, **historical**, **disputed**, **inaccessible**, and **unknown**. A facility name, map point, design capacity, project announcement, or legal label is not automatically evidence of current operation or usable capacity.

The Demonstrator 01 correction is especially important. The difference between reported running capacity and reported output is a **partial-observation field difference**, not measured lost material, rejects, residuals, pollution, or entropy. The unobserved middle is preserved explicitly in the unknown-process register.

## How to use the GIS

Open GeoJSON and Shapefile layers in QGIS. Open CSV files in LibreOffice Calc, Excel, or a GIS as point/table data where coordinates are provided. The GIS package README explains CRS, source links, dates, field meaning, and the limitations of OSM and NSDI layers.

Do not interpret OSM mapping coverage as an official facility inventory. Do not interpret proximity as contamination, illegality, hydrological linkage, or causal evidence. Do not combine records with incompatible periods, boundaries, units, or denominators.

## How to reproduce analyses

The scripts are included, but downloaded source files and their dates should be checked before rerunning. No simulated or random data are used. Derived outputs must preserve source, period, boundary, unit, method, evidence grade, and uncertainty.

## Current maturity

**Freedom from Waste** is the most developed domain, with national and Western Province evidence, GIS exploration, facility-capacity research, hidden-dumping analysis, and Demonstrator 01. **Material Afterlives** has a defined conceptual architecture but needs a separate desk-based source inventory. **Ecological Flourishing** is deliberately separate and needs its own ecological condition, restoration, and persistence architecture.

## Package caveat

This is a versioned research package, not a claim that Sri Lanka’s current facility system is fully known. Several important databases and current operational records remain inaccessible or unavailable in public form. Unknowns and source conflicts are part of the research result and should not be removed for presentation convenience.
