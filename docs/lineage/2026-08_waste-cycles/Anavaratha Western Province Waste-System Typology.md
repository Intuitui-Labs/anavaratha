# Anavaratha Western Province Waste-System Typology

**Status:** Desk-based provisional typology, 22 August 2026. **Purpose:** Classify system types before selecting local cases. These are analytical types, not official administrative classifications.

## Typology

| Type | Defining configuration | Evidence basis | Central reality question | Candidate verification focus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dense metropolitan transfer-dependent system | High population/activity density, limited land, urban transfer points, long-distance movement to treatment or disposal | CMC, Kolonnawa, Wattala, Peliyagoda, Kotte, Maharagama sections; WMA plan | Does transfer infrastructure prevent dumping or merely relocate ground handling and residual burden? | Transfer-station ground conditions, routing, spillages, destination logs |
| Centralized large-site disposal and rehabilitation system | Large facility receives regional waste, carries legacy stock, and is subject to refurbishment or technology projects | Karadiyana, Site A, WMA/JICA intervention | Does rehabilitation create controlled operation or only improve part of a continuing dump system? | Current polygon, intake, stockpile, leachate, equipment uptime, residuals |
| Emergency-diversion and adjacent-temporary-disposal system | Formal treatment facility coexists with temporary disposal on nearby land during crisis | Post-Meethotamulla Kerawalapitiya/SLLRDC pathway | Do emergency areas remain after the formal emergency ends? | Adjacent polygons, closure/remediation, later land use, waste traces |
| Distributed local-authority open-dump system | Many small or medium LA sites, often constrained by land, fleet, staff, and finance | 21 WMA open-dump map leads; 22 LA-operated disposal sites | Are small sites visible in national inventories and are they actually closed or merely renamed? | Map-lead georeferencing, current status, permit, overflow and burning |
| Compost-and-residual system | Organic treatment is formally present, but contamination, low production, rejects, and residual disposal remain | 28 compost facilities; 22-station 2022 dataset; WMA plan | How much waste is truly transformed and how much becomes residual stock or disposal? | Input quality, output, rejects, product stock, residual destination |
| Private-outsourced disposal system | Local authorities without land contract private disposal or shared facilities | WMA plan states 10 LAs outsource disposal | Does outsourcing improve environmental performance or transfer accountability and burden? | Contract, receiving site, weighbridge, licence, inspection, route |
| Planned-network and technology-transition system | Future transfer stations, TRFs, waste parks, compost upgrades, and Aruwakkalu links are planned or staged | WMA 2022–2042 capacity tables and action schedules | Which planned assets were actually built, commissioned, maintained, and used? | Procurement, completion, commissioning, actual throughput and persistence |

## Typology caution

The same place may belong to more than one type at different times. Karadiyana can be a large centralized disposal site, a rehabilitation-transition site, a compost-and-residual site, and a conflicted-status site simultaneously. The type must therefore be attached to a period and evidence source rather than treated as a permanent identity.

## Controlled descent candidates

The strongest first candidates for later controlled field validation are not selected because they are presumed worst. They are selected because they test different mechanisms:

| Candidate | Hypothesis to test | Why not yet final |
|---|---|---|
| Karadiyana | Rehabilitation/equipment inputs may coexist with non-sanitary practice or residual leakage | Current permits, polygons, throughput, and environmental evidence unresolved |
| Kerawalapitiya adjacent area | Emergency disposal beside a formal waste park may persist or leave legacy burden | Exact temporary area and present condition unresolved |
| Dompe | A formal sanitary-landfill label may not reveal current operation and residual pathways | Current status and monitoring records not publicly assembled |
| One WMA map lead in dense north/central corridor | Small or medium LA disposal may remain hidden inside distributed urban systems | Numbered map symbols need georeferencing and current authority confirmation |
| One southern/interior WMA map lead | Land scarcity and dispersed service may produce different disposal pathways | Site identity and current status unresolved |

No candidate should be treated as an allegation. Selection requires a current source, defensible geometry, a prior hypothesis, and an actionable verification question.
