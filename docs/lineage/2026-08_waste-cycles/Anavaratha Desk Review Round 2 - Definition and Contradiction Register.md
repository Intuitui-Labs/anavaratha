# Anavaratha Desk Review Round 2: Definition and Contradiction Register

## Purpose

Sri Lankan waste sources use similar words for different measurement boundaries. This register prevents the desk review from treating unlike quantities as a single baseline.

## Definition register

| Term | Questions that must be answered before comparison |
|---|---|
| Waste generated | Does it include only municipal solid waste, all solid waste, commercial waste, industrial waste, or self-disposed material? |
| Waste collected | Collected by local authorities only, or also private operators and informal actors? Does it include street, market, and institutional waste? |
| Waste recovered | Does it include reuse, informal recovery, recycling, composting, material sold, or only material formally processed? |
| Controlled disposal | What facility-control criteria are applied? Is a sanitary landfill treated differently from an open dump or managed disposal site? |
| Uncollected waste | Does it include self-disposal, burning, burial, illegal dumping, leakage, or only waste not recorded by local authorities? |
| Plastic waste | Does it include packaging, industrial scrap, e-waste plastics, textiles, thermosets, laminated plastics, or only post-consumer municipal plastic? |
| Capacity | Nominal design capacity, operating capacity, usable capacity, or capacity after maintenance/contamination/access restrictions? |
| Local authority | Municipal council, urban council, pradeshiya sabha, provincial authority, or another administrative unit? |
| Recycling | Collection, sorting, reprocessing, sale, or final production of a new product? |
| Current | Publication year, measurement year, policy target year, or latest available estimate? |

## Quantitative contradiction register

| Record | Source and value | Why it conflicts or cannot be merged directly | Correct handling |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q01 | National Action Plan, 2021 report: around 10,768 tonnes/day of all solid waste generated and 3,458 tonnes/day collected | National estimate; wording is “all solid waste” in the report’s executive summary; reference data are historical and may include different boundaries | Preserve as the plan’s baseline; do not label as a current MSW value without definition review |
| Q02 | Basnayake et al., 2019 article preview: generation of 7,210 tonnes/day and collection efficiency of 27.2% | Different source, period, boundary, and calculation; may refer to municipal solid waste rather than all solid waste | Record as an independent historical estimate and compare methods, not totals alone |
| Q03 | IGES publication page for the plastic action plan: estimated plastic generation around 938 tonnes/day, collection around 300 tonnes/day, recycling around 38 tonnes/day | Plastic-only flow; not comparable with total solid waste; plan estimate and reference period must be retained | Use for plastic lifecycle structure and historical scenario analysis |
| Q04 | Plastic action plan text: approximately 47.7% of plastic waste openly burnt in individual premises | Specific mechanism and source estimate; may overlap with “uncollected” categories and cannot be added to them without checking the flow diagram | Code as a pathway estimate; inspect the report’s mass-balance figure before aggregation |

## Non-quantitative contradictions to investigate

The literature alternates between explanations centered on public awareness, technical capacity, policy, political leadership, infrastructure, markets, and data. These are not necessarily mutually exclusive. They may operate at different links in the same chain or become binding under different local conditions. Anavaratha should therefore ask which constraint binds first, for which actor, at which scale, and during what period.

The policy literature supports prevention, source separation, circularity, and resource recovery, while some proposed plans emphasize thermal recovery or end-of-pipe infrastructure. This is a policy-priority tension rather than a simple factual contradiction. The desk review should compare the expected behavior, residuals, environmental controls, costs, and incentives of each approach.

## Contradiction protocol

When a new source conflicts with an existing record, create a new entry rather than editing the old one. Record source, page, value or claim, unit, method, boundary, period, actor, and likely reason for disagreement. Classify the contradiction as definition, time, space, sampling, reporting incentive, model assumption, or unresolved. A contradiction can be resolved only when the underlying definitions and evidence are compatible.
