## 1. Purpose

This document extracts only claims that can be connected to plant identity, medicinal-plant knowledge, threatened flora, and the Osu Uyana line of inquiry. It does not reproduce the full catalogues. It records what the existing sources can support and what still requires access, verification, or ethical engagement.

## 3. What is directly reusable

The National Herbarium page can be used as an institutional anchor for plant authentication, taxonomy, flora documentation, and threatened-plant research [1]. The 2025 checklist can serve as a high-value baseline for medicinal-plant names, synonyms, conservation status, use patterns, and practitioner-survey context [2]. The 2025 article is published in the *Ceylon Journal of Science* and is marked CC Attribution 4.0 on its journal page [2].

The sources can support a source-linked atlas, but they do not remove the need for local ecological context, current status verification, or consent-based knowledge work.

## 4. What should not be inferred

The reported national medicinal-plant totals should not be treated as a complete or timeless list of Sri Lankan medicinal plants. “Used for medicine” does not by itself establish clinical efficacy, safe dosage, legal permission, or sustainable harvest. A threatened medicinal plant is not automatically a cultivation target. A plant’s inclusion in a checklist does not establish its habitat, pollinator, soil, shade, water, or regeneration relationships.

The term **Osu Uyana** should not be treated as fully defined from one secondary reference. Its species, institutional forms, local meanings, language use, knowledge holders, and relationship to Ayurvedic or indigenous practice require further verification.

## 6. Next extraction priorities

The next step should obtain the full species tables from the 2025 checklist, identify the fields available for names, synonyms, native status, endemicity, and conservation status, and create a crosswalk with National Herbarium identity resources. After that, a small subset of medicinal and food plants can be chosen for relationship records spanning habitat, seasonality, propagation, pollinators, soil, water, culture, and stewardship.

The project should also identify the responsible Sri Lankan authorities and knowledge holders for Osu Uyana-related research before publishing or repackaging any medicinal knowledge.

> **Reuse the catalogue for what it knows; do not make it claim what it does not know.**
