# Anavaratha

## Atlas prototype views

**Sri Lanka regenerative-cultivation dimension — Working views, Version 0.1**

## 1. Purpose

These views allow the prototype records to be read as a connected living system rather than as isolated entries. They are analytical views over the same source-linked records, not additional claims.

| Condition | Records affected | What existing sources help establish | What remains to be learned |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ecological fit** | All records | GAEZ, forest, homegarden, wetland, and species studies provide orientation | Local soil, water, elevation, habitat, and season |
| **Natural regeneration** | Trees, fruiting plants, threatened beings | Rainforest and mangrove restoration sources emphasise natural recovery and succession | Site-specific recruitment and intervention thresholds |
| **Seasonal continuity** | Pollinators, seed dispersers, food, medicinal plants | Homegarden and ecological studies provide local leads | Full flowering, fruiting, breeding, rainfall, and care calendars |

| **Knowledge continuity** | Medicinal and food plants, stewardship | Checklist and community studies identify practitioners and local practices | Consent, attribution, language, rights, and intergenerational transmission |
| **Stewardship capacity** | All records | Forest Department, DWC, CEA, universities, and community cases identify responsible actors | Actual local authority, resources, maintenance, and accountability |

## 5. Evidence view

| Evidence layer | Strongest current source | Prototype use | Confidence boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Plant identity** | National Herbarium and 2025 checklist | Names, synonyms, taxonomy, threatened flora | Verify record-level identity |
| **Medicinal status** | 2025 checklist | Native, endemic, threatened, use and practitioner leads | Preserve publication date and definitions |
| **Protected habitat** | DWC | Protected areas, corridors, permits, reference-site logic | Confirm current boundary and management data |
| **Wetland catalogue** | National Wetland Directory and CEA gateway | Existing wetland discovery | Assess date, completeness, and spatial precision |
| **Forest administration** | Forest Department | Responsible divisions and technical data pathways | Mandate is not evidence of field outcome |
| **Spatial orientation** | FAO GAEZ | Coarse Dry/Intermediate/Wet frame | Not sufficient for local ecological fit; no licence provided |
| **Threatened species** | Sri Lanka Biodiversity/CBD, IUCN | Status, range, basin and recovery lead | Global or historical ranges may be incomplete |
| **Pollination and homegardens** | University of Jaffna | Local relationship and cultivated-system context | Regional study; do not generalise nationally |
| **Soil life** | Sri Lankan AMF study | Root–fungus and soil-condition method | Experimental context limits transfer |
| **Restoration** | UN, IUCN, IWMI, peer-reviewed studies | Natural recovery, hydrology, participation | Case objectives are not measured outcomes |

## 6. Atlas reading sequence

A researcher should be able to move through the prototype in this order:

1. Select a living being, habitat, or relationship.
2. Trace its identity and source provenance.
3. Locate its habitat, ecological region, watershed, or cultivated context.
4. Identify associated beings and functions.
5. Record the conditions required for continuity.
6. Identify institutions, communities, and knowledge holders.
7. Review uncertainty, access, consent, and possible unintended effects.
8. State a research question or care hypothesis without prematurely turning it into an intervention.

## 7. Prototype test

The prototype passes its structural test if the following questions can be answered for each record:

| Test question | Required result |
|---|---|
| What is the entity? | Identity and source are traceable |
| Where is it? | Geography and scale are stated |
| What is it related to? | At least one relationship is documented or clearly marked as a lead |
| What conditions does it require? | Conditions are specific enough to investigate |
| Who cares or has authority? | Stewardship and institutional roles are visible |
| What is uncertain? | Gaps, limits, and conflicts are explicit |
| What should happen next? | Verification or research action is stated, not assumed |

> **A prototype record is useful when it makes the next question better.**
