# Anavaratha

## Cultivation Atlas Release 01

**Scope and composition — Version 0.1**

## 1. Purpose

Cultivation Atlas Release 01 is the first small, controlled demonstration of how Anavaratha can connect Sri Lankan living beings, habitats, relationships, seasons, institutions, and forms of care without becoming a replacement catalogue or a premature field programme.

The release is intentionally small. Its purpose is to test the quality of the connection rather than maximise the number of species. Each record must show not only **what exists**, but also **where, when, in what relationship, under which conditions, according to whose evidence, and with what uncertainty**.

> **Release 01 is a proof of method, not a claim of completeness.**

## 2. Release boundary

The release belongs exclusively to Anavaratha’s **regenerative ecological-cultivation direction**. It does not include waste flows, pollution management, clearance systems, or material-responsibility research. Those belong to the separate waste direction within the larger Anavaratha project home.

The release also does not provide planting prescriptions, medicinal treatment advice, propagation instructions, protected-species locations, or field-access commitments. It is a desk-research knowledge product whose possible future actions remain proportionate to evidence and governance.

## 3. Controlled composition

Release 01 should contain approximately eight to twelve controlled records across four record families:

| Record family | Intended representation | Evidence requirement |
|---|---|---|
| **Medicinal checklist examples** | A small number of source-validated medicinal-plant rows | Original Appendix 1 page, extracted fields, visual check, and explicit blank-field preservation |
| **Habitat and structural plants** | Trees or plants linked to habitat, shade, water, soil, coast, wetland, or regeneration | Independent ecological or institutional evidence beyond checklist inclusion |
| **Living relationships** | Pollination, frugivory, seed ingestion, soil symbiosis, shelter, or movement | Named organisms, relationship type, method, geography, and confidence |
| **Stewardship and governance** | Institutions, communities, gardens, protected areas, or knowledge holders | Source authority, access conditions, responsibility, and ethical limitations |

## 5. Minimum record schema

Every controlled record should contain the following fields:

| Field group | Required content |
|---|---|
| **Identity** | Name as sourced, normalised name if available, synonyms, family, local name, and taxonomic authority |
| **Place** | Country, ecological region, habitat, study site, spatial precision, and sensitive-location treatment |
| **Time** | Source date, observation period, season, phenology, status year, and whether the record is historical or current |
| **Relationship** | Other organisms, habitat functions, ecological interaction, cultural relation, or stewardship connection |
| **Condition** | Soil, water, shade, fruiting, flowering, movement, breeding, regeneration, or knowledge conditions where evidenced |
| **Evidence** | Source, method, original wording, page or section, licence, confidence, and direct-versus-interpreted distinction |
| **Governance** | Institution, community authority, access condition, permit requirement, consent, attribution, and ethical restriction |
| **Review state** | Accepted, merged, rejected, unresolved, source-ambiguous, or field-required |
| **Next verification** | The smallest responsible step needed to strengthen the record |

## 6. Inclusion rule

A record enters Release 01 only when its identity and source provenance are sufficiently clear for the limited claim being made. A plant checklist row can enter as a taxonomic and bibliographic record even when habitat, season, or care fields remain blank. An ecological relationship can enter only when the relationship, method, geography, and time are explicit.

> **A record is not strong because it has many fields. It is strong because every field knows what kind of evidence supports it.**

## 7. Exclusion rule

Release 01 excludes automatic full-checklist publication, unverified ancient Osu Uyana continuity, medicinal efficacy or safety claims, species-level cultivation prescriptions, sensitive coordinates, unsupported native/endemic inferences, generalized restoration recommendations, and any action that would require permits or community consent.

## 8. Success criterion

Release 01 succeeds if a reader can follow a small number of records from source to interpretation and see where knowledge is solid, where relationships are plausible, where evidence is missing, and what would be required before care or cultivation could responsibly be considered.
