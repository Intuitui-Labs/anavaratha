## 3. Provenance rule

Every prototype record retains its source-specific wording and scope. A record may be connected to another record through identity, spatial, temporal, functional, relational, institutional, or methodological links, but it should not be merged unless the match is exact or strongly verified.

## 4. Current prototype interpretation

The prototype shows that one living-system record may connect several research domains. Mango connects flowering, bees, food, household cultivation, and fruit production. A homegarden connects trees, medicinal plants, pollinators, soil, water, food, and culture. Red Fin Labeo connects species status to a basin, freshwater habitat, movement, recovery, and institutions. Kumbuk and Hora show why structural-tree selection must remain habitat-specific.

The prototype therefore tests Anavaratha’s central proposition:

> **The living unit is not an isolated species; it is a species or organism in relationship with habitat, season, other beings, people, and care.**
